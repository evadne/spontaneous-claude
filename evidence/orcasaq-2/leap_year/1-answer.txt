

Under the Gregorian calendar, a year is a leap year if:

- it is divisible by 4, **and**
- if it is a century year, it must also be divisible by 400.

So:

- **1900**: not a leap year (divisible by 4, but not by 400)
- **2000**: leap year (divisible by 400)
- **2100**: not a leap year (divisible by 4, but not by 400)
