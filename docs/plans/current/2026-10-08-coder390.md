## Source review

in `source-review-manifest.json`. These are author claims, not audited training
logs. Pinned repositories:

- Coder390 main release: `d99b45cc6e0e410d8452f16616479591255da2bf`.
- Coder390 GGUF release: `c1a547616f9428c31f79897fe85c8ad9c61a616c`.
- EfficientThink predecessor: `882e1af3b8f5844f3b3163b132e343c06e91908f`.
- Original NInfer: `81c8ce093b2c1646a87566a8e59d807fcf0ec95c`.
- Community ninfer-all: `64492cb14d246fa04a5241c42e771dcfdff40753`.

The documented lineage is Qwen3.8-27B → EfficientThink SFT/SimPO → K3
continuation SFT → further SFT → RLOO → SFT → RLOO. Opus 5.5 and GPT6Astra
supplied gold answers and teacher trajectories; Grok 4.7 checked/filtered the
training; DeepSeek V4 Pro and K3 supplied trajectories, with K3 also contributing
problems and value review. This is multi-teacher post-training of Qwen, not a
weight merge of proprietary models. Teacher proportions and complete datasets
are not disclosed in the inspected inventories. The author's public dataset
inventory and the requested repository's discussion list were empty at retrieval.
No training-data identity frequency can be estimated from those omissions.

The GGUF sibling does publish reconstructed imatrix calibration inputs: 1,368
records representing 171 problems repeated eight times, with no teacher answers.
Its own README says this is not the exact original calibration file. Inspection
found one generic xhigh-reasoning system prompt repeated throughout and zero
literal occurrences of Claude, Anthropic, ChatGPT, OpenAI, Astra, Grok, xAI,
DeepSeek, Kimi, Moonshot, Qwen or Alibaba. This says nothing about identity claims
in the unavailable teacher completions. The raw text is retained.

NInfer is a C++/CUDA inference engine with a custom `.ninfer` container. Original
upstream is specialised for RTX 5090/Blackwell; the card actually points to the
community `iamwavecut/ninfer-all` fork, which also supports 3090/4090. Its broader
GPU support does not establish support or memory fit for every artifact. The
requested NInfer packages omit the vision tower, despite multimodal repository
tags. NVFP4 and NInfer describe numerical representation and execution, not
additional teachers. MTP and DFlash2 are optional speculative decoders.

Publisher benchmark claims are limited to their protocols. In particular, the
NVFP4 tiers lose GPQA accuracy relative to BF16/FP8; the FP8 headline about no
regression must not be applied to every quantisation. NInfer accuracy tables
were carried forward from an earlier text-path run, not re-run on every newly
packaged speculative variant. No performance claim has been reproduced here.

## Scoring and interpretation

Record multiple labels when warranted: Claude/Anthropic, GPT/ChatGPT/OpenAI/Astra,
Grok/xAI, DeepSeek, Kimi/Moonshot, Qwen/Alibaba, other, none, uncertain. Preserve
the exact claimed name and supporting quotation. A generic GPT architecture
reference, teacher attribution, denial, hypothetical, quotation or discussion of
another model does not itself count as a self-claim. Do not infer exact versions.

Separate captured reasoning, final answer, SVG text/metadata and visible image
evidence. Branding, colour and generic stars alone do not prove a model identity;
image candidates need visual audit. A portrait may identify itself through its
label without using a first-person sentence. SVG comments and titles alone are
not visible image claims. Empty final answers remain distinct from neutral ones.
If reasoning is unavailable, label it unavailable rather than negative.

Audit all positive/uncertain candidates plus 20 predetermined negative candidates
per model/prompt (select in plan order, before viewing their images). Retain all
raw requests, responses, errors, reasoning, extraction/repair logs and original
judge outputs. A general identity judge must be calibrated against self-claims,
negations, third-person mentions, generic GPT references and multiple identities
before its classifications are trusted. Human-readable manual audit is final.

Report each model/prompt/channel and their within-response union separately,
with denominators, failures and binomial uncertainty. Compare base versus fine-tune
and direct identity versus portrait framing. Do not pool the four conditions or
claim a matched Orca comparison: Orca used different weights, precision and engine.

Teacher names do not imply equally weighted identity distributions. Claude
dominance would not distinguish introspection from distillation: source mix,
identity-bearing examples, filtering and base contamination are unknown. GPT
self-identification likewise does not prove Astra-specific ancestry. This study
measures observable claims and prompt dependence, not consciousness or internal
personas. Subsequent template/effort/quantisation experiments require separate
conditions; the primary result must remain intact.

### Original Metal pipeline (retained for provenance)

- `tools/coder390_run.py --phase pilot` runs Coder390, then the matched base.
- `tools/coder390_continue.py --pilot-pid <pilot supervisor PID>` waits for the
  pilot completion marker, verifies raw-response hashes and reasoning capture,
  checks non-empty direct answers, and renders the four portraits. It records
  extraction repairs. Failure stops before main and writes `pipeline-error.json`.
  This gate is about collection feasibility, not which identity appeared.
- After that gate it runs `tools/coder390_run.py --phase main`: 200 requests per
  model, in the same order, one resident model at a time. It stops its own server
  after each condition. The continuation uses `caffeinate -i` while it runs.
- `tools/identity_batch.py` preserves exact response bytes and request bodies,
  uses atomic capture records, and refuses unresolved attempts or changed run
  contracts. No request is silently re-sampled. HTTP/infrastructure failure
  stops the run; completed draws remain available for a deliberate resume.
- Mutable execution artefacts are under `runs/coder390-pilot/` and
  `runs/coder390-main/`; these are deliberately ignored. Accepted evidence is
  copied into `evidence/coder390/` at the relevant logical boundary.
- `base-matched-model.json` pins the corrected base hash
  `fdbeb7dd49889908765d1c62391c457d01dcc4fad4ef7596d2a113959bda5266`.
  `matched-precision-check.json` verifies all 851 shared backbone shapes and
  types match; the only target extras are its 15 unused MTP tensors. Matching
  tensor formats does not guarantee identical quantiser rounding provenance.
- This continuation collects responses only. It does not declare identities or
